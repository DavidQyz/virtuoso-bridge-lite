# AgentOS Lesson Candidates — virtuoso-bridge-lite

Stage 1 of the two-stage lesson-promotion flow. Record here any **generalizable
workflow lesson** (a reusable process insight, not project-specific design
knowledge) that might be worth promoting to the AgentOS hub.

Promotion to the hub requires explicit user approval. After approval, a report
is written to `GitHub/agentos/inbox/project_reports/virtuoso-bridge/`.

What belongs here: reusable process patterns (e.g. "verify a copied OA cell by
diffing si-generated netlists", "clear stale cross-host .cdslck locks before
edit"). What does NOT: IC design conclusions, sizing, sim results — those stay
in the per-device READMEs and Claude memory.
